Career history

Term 0: Background

You were born Aslan, raised on Drinax (In service of King Oleb). A servant of the Kingdom of Drinax, raised amid the decaying wonders of the citadel, trained to help restore the kingdom to former glory. Growing up, you picked up the fundamentals of Drive, Flyer, and Mechanic. You loved driving/flying vehicles as a teenager and used to fix them after occasional crashes. You had good and close relationship with your sibling, but were bullied during your early school years.
